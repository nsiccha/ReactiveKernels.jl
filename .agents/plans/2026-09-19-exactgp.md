## The two functions (`DistributionKernelSources`, plain Julia, NOT @kernel)

- `gp_exp_quad_cov(x::AbstractVector, sigma::Real, rho::Real,
  jitter::Real)`: Stan `gp_exp_quad_cov` iso math
  (`σ²exp(−(xᵢ−xⱼ)²/2ρ²)`, jitter on the diagonal), validated args
  (positive sigma/rho, finite nonneg jitter, n≥1). Matrix `x` →
  explicit "aniso sequenced" error (fallback method, tested).
- `gp_chol_latent(K::AbstractMatrix, z::AbstractVector)`: pure-Julia
  lower potrf (NO LAPACK — Enzyme cannot differentiate LAPACK; this is
  the native-path enabler) + lower-triangular matvec. Square/length
  checks + explicit "not positive definite (increase jitter)" error.
- Native path ONLY (approved non-goal: Reactant reverse through dense
  Cholesky is upstream-blocked). Written traceably (broadcasts/loops);
  XLA stance retested when the upstream gap closes.
